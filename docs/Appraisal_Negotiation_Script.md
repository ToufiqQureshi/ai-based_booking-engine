# Professional Appraisal Negotiation Script

## 1. The Opening (The "Bridge" to Value)
"Sir, maine pichle 8 mahino mein company ke marketing aur technology side pe kaafi deeply kaam kiya hai. Main aaj aapse apni progress aur company ke future roadmap (Staybooker) pe baat karna chahta hoon."

## 2. The Value Proposition (Highlighting the "Asset")
"Maine 'Staybooker' ko ek idea se lekar ek live product tak akele build kiya hai.
- Aaj hamare paas ek **100% In-house SaaS Product** hai jo humein 'Service Agency' se 'Product Company' banata hai.
- Ismein AI Concierge, Real-time Pricing, aur Multi-property management jaise features hain jo market mein top-tier software mein hote hain.
- Iske saath-saath, mera autonomous scraper 1000+ URLs se competitor pricing fetch kar raha hai, aur main 4 websites ka SEO akele handle kar raha hoon."

## 3. The Comparison (Team vs Solo)
"Sir, Staybooker jaisa product build karne ke liye market mein ek puri team lagti hai—Product Manager, Frontend/Backend Devs, aur QA.
Us team ki monthly cost kam se kam **₹2.5 se ₹3 Lakhs** hoti.
Maine ye sab akele, pichle 8 mahino mein deliver kiya hai, jisse company ke **₹20 Lakhs se zyada** bache hain."

## 4. The Ask (The Number)
"Meri current salary ₹20k hai, jo ki mere delivers aur market value ke hisaab se kaafi kam hai. Is product ki ownership, maintenance, aur future growth ko dekhte huye, main **₹60,000 per month** expect kar raha hoon."

---

## 5. Handling Objections (Rebuttals)

### Objection: "Tumne pehle inform kyun nahi kiya ki 8 logon ka kaam hai?"
**Rebuttal:** "Sir, main starting mein feasibility check kar raha tha. Main nahi chahta tha ki main aapse koi bada vaada karoon jo main deliver na kar paun. Maine extra hours aur weekend lagakar isse proof-of-concept se lekar live product banaya hai taaki main aapko results dikha sakun, sirf baatein nahi."

### Objection: "Ye toh tumhare KRA (Data Scientist) mein hi tha?"
**Rebuttal:** "Sir, Data Scientist ka KRA data analyze karke insights dena hota hai. Lekin Staybooker ek pura **Software Ecosystem** hai. Backend architecture, Frontend UI, Security, Payments, aur AI integration—ye sab Engineering ka kaam hai jo maine initiative lekar kiya hai taaki company ka revenue scale ho sake."

### Objection: "20k se sidha 60k? Itna hike nahi de sakte."
**Rebuttal:** "I understand sir, % wise ye bada lagta hai. Lekin agar aap **Value vs Cost** dekhein, toh ₹60k mein aapko ek Developer, ek SEO Head, aur ek Data Scientist mil raha hai. Bahar ye kaam karwane ke liye aapko ₹3 Lakh/month kharch karne padenge. 60k company ke liye abhi bhi ek bahut badi saving hai."

---

## 6. If they say "NO" (The Middle Ground / Plan B)
Agar wo bilkul nahi mante:
1. **Stepped Hike:** "Sir, agar abhi possible nahi hai, toh kya hum ise steps mein kar sakte hain? ₹45k immediate aur 3 mahine baad performance dekh kar ₹60k?"
2. **Profit Sharing:** "Kya hum base salary + Staybooker se aane wale revenue ka kuch percentage (commission) fix kar sakte hain?"
3. **Professional Respect:** "Sir, mujhe aapke saath kaam karna pasand hai, lekin meri market value aur responsibility ke beech ka gap bahut zyada hai. Main chahta hoon ki hum ek aise point pe pahunchein jahan mera aur company dono ka grow hona sustain ho sake."
