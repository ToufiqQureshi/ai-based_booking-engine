About The Resort

Discover the Unseen
Beauty

Noune resort is an idyllic resort situated in the range of the Naga Hills. Nestled by the tranquil waters of Noune Lake, our resort offers a perfect blend of traditional hospitality and modern luxury. Wake up to the sound of birds, walk by the pristine lake, and find your inner peace.
